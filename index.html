Flying In

The nearest airport is Miami International (MIA), about 40 minutes from the venue.

Getting Around

Homestead is fairly rural, so Uber/Lyft can take a little longer to arrive than they would in the city — we'd suggest requesting your ride a few extra minutes early, especially leaving the venue at the end of the night.
